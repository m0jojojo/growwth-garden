Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Growwth Garden collects through growwthgarden.com, why we collect it and what we do with it. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Who we are

Growwth Garden is a marketing agency based in Delhi, India. You can reach us at growwthgarden@gmail.com or on +91-9369033958.

Information we collect

  • Contact form. Your name, email address, telephone number, country, subject and message, which you choose to send us.
  • Emails and calls. Any details you share when you email or phone us.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider and the services described below may receive your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, as part of normal web traffic.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to your inquiry, understand your business needs, prepare proposals and provide our services. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use the contact form to send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

Services that handle data

To run this website we rely on trusted providers, and your data may pass through them:

  • Cloudflare. Hosts and delivers the website.
  • Resend. Delivers contact form messages to our email inbox.
  • Google Maps. Loads the map on the contact page, so Google may receive your IP address when you open that page.

If we add analytics or advertising tools, we will update this policy to say so.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and manage any resulting work, and for a reasonable period afterwards for our business records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it or delete it. To do so, email us at growwthgarden@gmail.com and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Security

The website is served over HTTPS, and contact form messages are sent to us through an encrypted connection. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our website and services are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Questions about your data?

Get in touch and we will be glad to help.
