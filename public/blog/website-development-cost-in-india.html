Website creation cost in India ranges from ₹10,000 for a simple template site to ₹20,00,000+ for a custom web application. This guide breaks down every price: by website type, platform, page count, hiring option, hosting and the costs people forget.

The short answer

  • Simple template or DIY site: ₹10,000 – ₹30,000 (builders from ₹79 – ₹1,500 per month).
  • Professional small business website (WordPress, 5–15 pages): ₹50,000 – ₹2,00,000. This is the realistic range for a mobile-responsive, SEO-ready site.
  • Corporate website: ₹1,20,000 – ₹6,00,000.
  • E-commerce store: ₹80,000 – ₹8,00,000+, depending on products and features.
  • Custom web app / SaaS: ₹3,00,000 – ₹20,00,000+.
  • Running costs after launch: about ₹20,000 – ₹2,00,000 per year for a small business (domain, hosting, SSL, maintenance, content).

If you searched for "website creation cost in India", you have probably noticed that quotes range from ₹3,800 to ₹30 lakh for what sounds like the same thing. The gap is real, and it is explainable. Price depends on what you are building, who builds it, and what is included.

We compared the pricing published by five leading Indian hosting, development and design companies, and kept the figures and advice that they agree on. Where sources disagree, we show a range instead of a single number. All prices are in Indian rupees (₹), exclude 18% GST unless stated, and reflect 2027 market rates.

Static, one-page and basic business sites

A static site has fixed pages that rarely change. A five-page static site that includes a domain and hosting can cost as little as ₹5,000 – ₹8,000, and a one-page site up to about ₹8,000 – ₹20,000 on a template. A custom landing page built for conversions costs ₹15,000 – ₹60,000. Basic business sites usually run on WordPress with pre-built themes.

Standard business and corporate sites

Standard sites have 10–20 custom pages, a professional UI/UX, and features such as appointment booking, quote forms and CRM integration. Corporate sites add brand-led design, case studies, sub-pages and integrations. Each integration (booking, CRM, payment gateway, live chat, customer portal, multi-language) is effectively a mini-project.

Custom web applications

Custom builds (React, Node, Laravel and similar) are priced by features: user roles and permissions, data complexity, third-party integrations, real-time features, reporting, scalability and compliance. Expect a team of senior developers, a UI/UX designer, QA and a project manager. Plan for ₹3,00,000 – ₹25,00,000+ and 3–12 months, with complex platforms going far beyond.

11Hidden costs most people miss

  • Domain, hosting and SSL renewals: annual renewal prices are often higher than the first-year offer.
  • Premium themes and plugins: ₹3,000 – ₹20,000 per year for SEO, forms, caching and security plugins.
  • Third-party tools: email marketing, CRM, live chat, analytics and marketing automation, plus 2–3% payment gateway fees.
  • AI and LLM usage: chatbots, AI search and other AI features on your site call an LLM API that bills per token, so the cost grows with every visitor message and is not part of the build quote. Set usage limits and a monthly budget alert before launch.
  • Content: copywriting, photography and video are usually not included in the quote.
  • Post-launch changes: billed separately after sign-off, and mid-project changes can cost 3–5x more than planning them upfront.
  • Compliance and legal: privacy policy and terms (₹10,000 – ₹50,000), accessibility (₹30,000 – ₹1,50,000) and GDPR (₹50,000 – ₹2,00,000) if you serve international users.
  • GST: 18% on top of most agency quotes.
  • Hosting upgrades: as traffic grows, you will outgrow shared hosting.
  • Ongoing SEO and ads: a one-time build does not drive traffic on its own.

Total running cost: small businesses typically spend about ₹20,000 – ₹2,00,000 per year after launch, and more for enterprise sites.

14How to reduce your website development cost

  1. Start with an MVP. Launch core pages and features first, then iterate using real user feedback.
  2. Use proven platforms. Established CMSs, popular frameworks and pre-built themes or components cut build time sharply.
  3. Lock requirements before development. Document pages, features, user stories and mockups. Changes made mid-project can cost 3–5x more.
  4. Prepare your own content. Supplying copy, photos and logos removes a large line item and avoids delays.
  5. Use free plugins first. Only buy premium plugins when a free one genuinely cannot do the job.
  6. Pick the right hosting. Do not overbuy dedicated or cloud hosting on day one, but do not put a growing store on the cheapest shared plan either.
  7. Choose a long-term partner. A consistent team gives better rates, smoother maintenance and faster fixes.
  8. Get multiple quotes. Compare scope, not just price, and confirm what is included.

15How to budget and choose a website partner

If you would rather have a team scope it for you, our website, app and AI development service starts with a clear, itemised quote.

Set a realistic budget

  • Budget at least ₹25,000 – ₹50,000 for a basic professional build, and ₹50,000 – ₹2,00,000 for a business site that supports marketing.
  • Allocate roughly 20–30% to design, 50–60% to development, and the remainder to content, hosting and testing.
  • Add a 20% contingency for scope changes.
  • Budget separately for domain, hosting, content, maintenance, design and SEO.

Checklist before you sign

  • Define scope, audience, goals and timeline before requesting quotes.
  • Review portfolios, case studies and client reviews.
  • Check communication, responsiveness and technical depth.
  • Ask whether SEO, content, SSL, hosting, training and post-launch support are included.
  • Ask for milestones, transparent pricing, and a full handoff (source files, documentation, deployment support).
  • Confirm the pricing model: fixed project cost, hourly rate or monthly retainer.
  • Confirm whether the quote includes GST.
  • Prefer mobile-friendly design, fast loading and simple navigation. Design quality strongly shapes trust: research cited in industry guides suggests around 75% of consumers judge credibility from design.

    17Frequently asked questions

    What is the average website development cost in India in 2027?

    A professional, mobile-responsive WordPress business website typically costs ₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000. Very simple template sites start at ₹10,000 to ₹30,000, e-commerce stores run from ₹80,000 to ₹8,00,000 or more, and custom web applications start around ₹3,00,000 to ₹5,00,000 and can exceed ₹20,00,000.

    How much does a website cost for a small business in India?

    Most small businesses spend ₹50,000 to ₹1,50,000 with an agency for a 5 to 15 page CMS website. Freelancers often quote ₹15,000 to ₹70,000 for similar scope, but the quote may exclude design, content or post-launch support.

    What is the cheapest way to build a website in India?

    A DIY website builder is the cheapest route, with plans from roughly ₹79 to ₹1,500 per month, or a template-based package from ₹3,800 to ₹30,000. The trade-off is a generic look, limited functionality and limited scalability.

    Freelancer vs agency: which is cheaper for website development?

    Freelancers are cheaper, at about ₹500 to ₹2,000 per hour or ₹15,000 to ₹80,000 per small business site. Small agencies charge about ₹1,000 to ₹2,000 per hour or ₹80,000 to ₹3,00,000 per project, but they offer a team, a structured process and post-launch support.

    How much does an e-commerce website cost in India?

    A basic Shopify or WooCommerce store with up to 100 products costs ₹80,000 to ₹3,00,000. A mid-size store with 500 or more products, multiple payment options and custom design costs ₹3,00,000 to ₹7,00,000. Multi-vendor marketplaces start around ₹6,00,000 to ₹10,00,000 and can go beyond ₹20,00,000.

    How long does it take to build a website in India?

    A basic business website takes 2 to 4 weeks, a standard business website 4 to 8 weeks, a corporate or mid-size e-commerce site 6 to 14 weeks, and a custom web application 3 to 6 months or more. Delays in content, approvals and scope changes extend these timelines.

    What are the ongoing costs of a website in India?

    Plan for domain renewal (₹500 to ₹2,000 per year, more for premium names), hosting (₹2,000 to ₹50,000 per year), SSL (free to ₹10,000 per year), maintenance (₹15,000 to ₹60,000 per year or 15 to 20 percent of the build cost), premium plugins, business email, and optional SEO and marketing. Small businesses typically spend ₹20,000 to ₹2,00,000 per year in total.

    Should I choose WordPress or custom development?

    Choose WordPress for most standard business sites, blogs and portfolios. It typically costs 50 to 70 percent less than a comparable custom build. Choose custom development when you need unique backend logic, user roles, APIs, high performance or strict security.

    Why do website quotes in India vary so much?

    Quotes vary with design complexity, page count, features and integrations, technology stack, developer experience and location, timeline, and whether content, SEO, hosting and maintenance are included. A very low quote usually means a pre-built template with limited customisation.

    Is GST charged on website development in India?

    Yes. Website development is an IT service and attracts GST, currently 18 percent. Always ask whether a quote is inclusive or exclusive of GST, because it adds noticeably to the final bill.

    Final thoughts

    Website development cost in India in 2027 is not one number. It is a range shaped by what you build, how you build it and who builds it. For most businesses, ₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000 buys a professional, fast, SEO-ready website, and ₹20,000 to ₹2,00,000 a year keeps it secure, found and converting. Define your goals, budget for the running costs, and compare quotes on scope and process, not price alone.
